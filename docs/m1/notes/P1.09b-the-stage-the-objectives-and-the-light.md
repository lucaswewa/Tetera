## Summary

Tetera has one interface for a stage, and the objectives and the light described.
- **The fake stage passes the stage's contract suite at either pace, and a fake broken on purpose fails it,** as the phase's *Done when* asks. A fake whose `wait` reported a move finished while it was still moving failed with "a move ends where it was sent".
- **The suite has tests of its own from the start:** a stage flawed on purpose, in one way for each check, fails that check.
- **`Stage`:**
  - capabilities: each axis's travel and fastest speed, its resolution, where it homes;
  - readings of where it is and what it is doing, timestamped;
  - moves to and by, jogs, stop, homing; each starts and returns, and `wait` waits until the stage is still;
  - grblHAL's states, as events.
- **The objectives:** the catalogue of the four Mitutoyo objectives in `configs/objectives.toml`, each checked, and a mount where the user says which is fitted.
- **The light:** its channels, each with its intensity and colour temperature checked, and a manual light that records what the user says of it.
- **[ADR-0013](../../adr/0013-the-hardware-abstraction-layer.md)** gains the stage's design: moves that start and return.

## What was built

- **In `tetera-hal`:**
  - `stage`: `Axis`; `AxisCapabilities` and `StageCapabilities`, with `check_target` and `check_speed`; `StageState`, `StageReading` and `StageEvent`; the `Stage` trait, its `move_by` written once for every stage from `reading` and `move_to`;
  - `fake::stage`: `FakeStage`, 100 × 75 mm of travel in x and y at 10 mm/s, 10 mm in z at 2 mm/s, homing to the origin; its moves and homing at once or in time (`Pace`), on a straight line, each axis kept to its fastest; `StageFault`: a refused move, a stall into alarm, an alarm, a disconnection and its return;
  - `contract::stage`: eight checks every stage must pass, and `all`;
  - `objective`: `Objective`, `ObjectiveCatalogue` (from TOML, checked), `ObjectiveEvent`, the `ObjectiveMount` trait and `ManualObjectiveMount`;
  - `illumination`: `LightChannel`, `IlluminationCapabilities` with `check`, `IlluminationEvent`, the `Illumination` trait and `ManualIllumination`. A channel's state is `tetera-core`'s `LightState`, the one frames carry.
- **`configs/objectives.toml`:** the 5×, 10×, 20× and 50× Mitutoyo M Plan Apo objectives, with their numerical apertures and working distances (Q24).
- **The `hal-fakes` example** drives the fake stage, reads the catalogue, fits an objective and sets the light.
- **A new dependency:** `toml` 1.2.1, with `toml_parser` held at 1.1.5, each released before the two-week cutoff.

## Design notes and deviations from the plan

- **Moves start and return; `wait` waits.** A scan can expose while the stage settles, and `stop` can come from another thread while a move is under way. `wait` gives the reading once the stage is still: an error if it ended in alarm.
- **States are grblHAL's:** idle, moving, jogging, homing, and alarm, which only homing clears. A move while the stage moves is `Busy`; in alarm, a `Hardware` error.
- **Speeds:** a move's speed is along its path, and each axis is kept to its own fastest; a speed at or below zero is refused. A jog's sign is its direction.
- **The fake's motions:** a straight line from where it set off, in proportion to the time, worked out when read. A thread for each motion ends it on time, unless it is cut short: `stop`, a stopped jog, an alarm, or dropping the stage.
- **Jogs take their time at either pace;** only moves and homing are instant at `Pace::Instant`.
- **The suite runs from where the stage is:** it homes a stage in alarm first, and steps 100 µm in from each axis's end, so it runs on a real stage anywhere in its travel. Its tests run it from the origin, from the far end of the travel, and from an alarm.
- **The objectives are dry,** as the Mitutoyo ones are: a numerical aperture between 0 and 1. The catalogue refuses fields it doesn't know.
- **No interface for the objectives or the light is a contract suite:** both are manual for now, and their unit tests cover them. The virtual light (P1.12) may want one.

## Tests, and the mutants

- **32 new tests, 52 in the crate,** in every build:
  - **the stage's types:** each axis's coordinate; targets beyond the travel and speeds at or below zero refused, saying which; which states are still; capabilities, readings and events through JSON;
  - **the fake stage:**
    - the contract suite, at either pace;
    - a move's duration, from its slowest axis or its speed;
    - where a motion is, in proportion to the time;
    - a timed move under way on its line, then arriving, and its events;
    - a jog to the end of the travel;
    - busy while moving, and a wait no longer than asked;
    - each fault;
    - an alarm holding until homing, on a fresh stage and during a jog;
    - a disconnection ending a wait;
    - dropping the stage ending its motion's thread;
  - **the suite's own tests:**
    - it runs from an alarm and from the far end of the travel;
    - a stage flawed on purpose fails each check. The flaws: no z axis; `wait` reporting a move done early; silence; a move by taken as a move to; targets clamped into the travel; `stop` ignored; `stop_jog` ignored; homing ignored; failing to stop when still. One more runs `all` on a stage whose last check fails;
  - **the objectives:** the shipped catalogue, through `from_toml` and `load`; each thing wrong with a catalogue said, 15 cases; a missing file, a folder and a bad file, each saying which; the user fitting an objective, and a name not in the catalogue refused;
  - **the light:** each thing wrong with a light's state said, 7 cases and an unknown channel; the manual light recording what the user says.
- **The contract suite's checks:**
  - sound capabilities: x, y and z, home and the stage within the travel, through JSON;
  - a move ends where it was sent, idle, stays there, and says it set off and arrived;
  - a move by is relative;
  - a target beyond the travel, or a speed at or below zero, refused, and nothing moved;
  - a stop stills a move;
  - a jog runs until stopped, refusing a move meanwhile;
  - homing ends at home, idle;
  - stopping a still stage does nothing.
- **Broken on purpose,** as the spec asks: **a fake that reports a move finished before it is,** its `wait` returning while it still moved. The suite caught it: "a move ends where it was sent". It stays as a test, the flaw *reports done early*.
- **Run 15 times,** the tests passed each time.
- **Mutants:** 197 in the phase's diff; **141 caught, 51 unviable, 5 timed out, none missed.**
  - **Timed out:** with a motion that never ends (`finish`, its thread, or a duration made far too long), the suite waits, and the tests hang until the time limit.
  - **The first run missed 21:**
    - **Untested edges:** a working distance of zero; a stall in z; a duration between two points off the origin; where a motion is, partway. Each has a test now.
    - **Never run:** the suite's paths for a stage in alarm, or at the far end of its travel. Both now run.
    - **The motion's generation:** it was changed as motions started and ended, though only a motion cut short needs it. It changes only then now; an alarm on a fresh stage and during a jog tests it.
  - **Three exclusions, each with its reason in `.cargo/mutants.toml`:**
    - two in the suite's step, each another target within the travel, which tests a move as well;
    - the fake's jog direction at a speed of zero, which is refused first.
