## Summary

Recipes now run on the GPU without needless copies, and the live chain runs as the fused kernel.
- **A chain of CUDA nodes makes only the boundary copies,** as the phase's *Done when* asks. On a 20 MP frame, with the dark and the flat held, each run of the live chain on CUDA copies the frame up and the preview down, 47 MB, and nothing else, fused or not.
- **Fusion triggers, and its output matches the unfused recipe's,** exactly. The fusion pass finds `[bin →] [dark →] [flat →] demosaic → resize → colour` in a recipe, and runs P1.06b's fused kernel in its place on the GPU.
- **Held inputs:** `Plan::hold` keeps an input, such as a master dark, from run to run, and the steps it alone feeds run once.
- **On a 20 MP frame,** a run of the live chain takes 130 to 160 ms on the CPU, 33 to 35 ms on CUDA node by node, and 30 ms fused. Before this phase, on CUDA it took 230 ms, each node copying its data to the GPU and back.

## What was built

- **`port`:** device values: `Value::DeviceRaw`, `DeviceU8`, `DeviceU16` and `DeviceF32`, each an `OnDevice`, shared between readers, and equal when it is the same buffer; `Value::upload` and `Value::download`, giving the bytes copied.
- **`run`,** the executor, moved out of `plan` and grown:
  - each value in a `Slot`, on the host, on the GPU, or both once copied;
  - a step on the GPU when its node declares CUDA and `Compute` has one; its frames and images copied up when they are on the host, and down for a step on the host;
  - `Copies`: each run's copies, both ways, and their bytes;
  - each value freed after its last reader;
  - `Plan::hold` and `Plan::release`; the steps that held inputs alone feed, kept from run to run, unless a node is stateful, and worked again when a held input changes or the backend does.
- **`fuse`:** the matcher, and the fused step: it makes the ISP's tables from the chain's parameters and the masters it is given, and makes them again only when the frame's layout or a master changes.
- **The raw path's nodes on the GPU:** binning, the superpixel, demosaicking, dark, flat, resize, colour and the display transform take values there and give their own there. Each keeps its tables on the GPU: the flat field once for each master, the resize for each size in, the colour and display tables once.
- **`Descriptor`:** `stateful` and `fusable`; and `Plan::fused`, `Plan::set_fusion`, and `Plan::ran`, which names the steps the last run ran, a fused chain as one.
- **The `recipe` example** runs the live chain on the CPU, on CUDA node by node, and fused, the dark and the flat held, and gives each run's copies.

## Design notes and deviations from the plan

- **Device values are known by their buffer.** Two `OnDevice` values are equal when they share one buffer. So a node can tell the master it got is the one it made its tables from, without reading it. The fused step and the flat node keep the master they saw, which keeps its buffer, and its address, from being reused.
- **Held inputs, added to the spec's scope.** On the GPU, the flat field needs its master on the host, and a master dark copied up every frame is a needless copy. Holding an input, and keeping what it alone feeds, makes a constant master cost nothing after the first run. P1.08b's notes left constant inputs to this phase.
- **Stateful nodes run every time.** A node that keeps state between runs, such as temporal averaging in P1.08d, says so, and runs even on held inputs.
- **The plan works its kept steps again for another backend:** values on the GPU are no use to a run on the CPU.
- **A step on the host between steps on the GPU** takes its values down, and the next step on the GPU takes them up again. The copy to the host is kept, so a second reader there doesn't copy again.
- **What the fusion pass fuses:**
  - binning fuses only by the mean, as the kernel bins; by the sum it stays a step of its own, and the chain fuses after it;
  - a chain whose middle is read elsewhere, by another step or as a recipe output, doesn't fuse, since the fused kernel never makes that middle;
  - an optional step read elsewhere ends the chain before it.
- **The fused step makes its tables on the host once** for each master: it copies the master dark and the master flat down to make them, then uploads its own. Those copies are the step's, made once, and aren't in the plan's count.
- **The Bayer split stays a host node:** its kernel's four planes come back as one buffer, which splitting on the GPU would copy anyway. It still uses the GPU through `Compute`, with copies of its own.

## Tests, and the mutants

- **8 new tests,** 4 in every build and 4 on the GPU. `tetera-pipeline` now has 37 without the feature, and 42 with it:
  - **the fusion matcher:** the live chain fuses whole; the chain fuses with or without its optional steps, binning by the mean fusing and by the sum not; a chain whose middle is read elsewhere doesn't, whether by a step or as an output; a recipe without the chain fuses nothing;
  - **held inputs:** the steps they alone feed run once, a stateful step every time, as `Plan::ran` shows; held again, worked again; let go, given again; holding an unknown input or a wrong type, and giving a held input, refused;
  - **on the GPU, the live chain fused and not:** its outputs exactly the CPU's; given everything, three frames up and the preview down; with the masters held, the first run the same, then only the frame up and the preview down, the flat's own dark subtraction not run again; a new master flat, then a new dark, each giving the CPU's preview for them; the same plan then on the CPU, its kept steps worked again there, with no copies;
  - **on the GPU, a value let go after its last reader:** a frame and an image tapped by test nodes, held by nothing else once their readers have run;
  - **on the GPU, device values:** every kind up and back, with its bytes; numbers, tables and JPEGs staying on the host; equal when the same buffer, not when another with the same values; and one resize step given two sizes in turn;
  - **on the GPU, a host step between GPU steps:** the image down for the crop, the crop up again once for the resize, though the statistics read it too, and the result the CPU's.
- **Broken on purpose,** as the spec asks: **the fusion matcher** without its check that each middle step feeds only the next. It failed the test of a chain whose middle is read elsewhere.
- **Mutants,** over this phase's changes:
  - **the first run, with CUDA:** 130 tested, 80 caught, 25 missed, 25 unviable. Most missed what no test observed:
    - whether fusion ran at all: `Plan::ran` now shows it;
    - whether a value was let go: tested now with taps;
    - a master changed under a kept table; a second size for resize; each upload and download; device values' equality;
    - the fused step's stub for a build without CUDA, which now isn't compiled at all;
    - two redundant conditions in the executor, removed.
  - **One exclusion, with its reason:** the fused step's check that its tables are current, made always false, only makes them again each run, the same preview, slower.
  - **The second run, with CUDA:** 133 tested, 103 caught, 26 unviable, 4 missed: the two excluded cache checks, whose exclusion missed the step's new module, now fixed; the CPU build's own `get`, which a CUDA build doesn't compile; and a held plan moved from the GPU to the CPU without a hold in between, now tested.
  - **Without CUDA,** the same diff: the run catches what a CPU build can run. The rest it misses is code it doesn't compile, or the GPU's own behaviour, which the CUDA run caught.
