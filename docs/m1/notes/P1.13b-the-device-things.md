## Summary

The virtual instrument's devices are Things over HTTP: `camera`, `stage`, `objective` and `illumination`, beside `system` and `simulation`. The phase's *Done when* holds:

- **Each Thing serves its Thing Description, and the OpenAPI document is complete:** every property and action of the four, 47 paths, beside the other Things'.
- **A move through HTTP takes the time the virtual stage says.**
  - **On a stepped clock,** a 500 µm move at 5 mm/s ends within the stage's next report of the move's own time, as its motion profile gives it.
  - **On the real server,** 10 mm at 20 mm/s took 0.606 s, against 0.6 s.
- **Watching `position` over WebSocket shows it change during the move:** the test sees it on its way, in order, to its end. Over server-sent events, the real server's move showed 31 positions in its 0.6 s.

## What was built

- **In `tetera-api`:**
  - **`stage`,** `StageThing`:
    - `position`, `state` and `moving`, observable, kept current by a task that reads the stage every 10 ms; `speed`, a setting; `capabilities`;
    - `move_to` and `move_by`, which end when the stage is still, with its reading, and stop it if cancelled;
    - `jog`, `stop_jog`, `halt`, `home`;
  - **`camera`,** `CameraThing`:
    - `capabilities`; `config`, and each setting a property: `mode`, `exposure` (ms), `gain`, `bit_depth`, `binning`, `roi`; `pixel_format`;
    - `white_balance`, a setting for the preview (P1.14a);
    - a stream to watch: `streaming`, `frames` (delivered and dropped), `last_frame`, `start_streaming` and `stop_streaming`;
  - **`objective`,** `ObjectiveThing`: `catalogue`, `fitted` (by name), `objective` (in full), and `remove`;
  - **`illumination`,** `IlluminationThing`: `capabilities`, `on`, `intensity`, `colour_temperature`;
  - **`error`:** a name a device doesn't have, written to a property, is a 422, as a bad value is;
  - **a stepped clock** for tests (`SimulatorConfig::stepped`), which the server's configuration refuses.
- **`crates/tetera-api/README.md`:** `curl` for each Thing, with a timed move.
- **No new dependencies.**

## Performance

- **The stage is read every 10 ms** while the server runs, to keep its properties current: a reading is a lock and a little arithmetic on the virtual stage. A real stage's driver (M5) reads its reports instead.
- **A move's invocation ends at the first report after the stage stops:** the virtual stage reports every 20 ms, 5 ms late, so a move ends up to 25 ms after the stage has stopped.

## Design notes and deviations from the plan

- **The stage's stop is called `halt`:** teta-wot gives every Thing a `stop` of its own, for the server to stop it, and an action can't share its name.
- **No objective is said with `remove`, not by writing `null` to `fitted`:** teta-wot takes a `null` body as a missing one, as FastAPI does, so a property can't be written to `null`. The same holds for the camera's `roi`, written `null` for the whole sensor: the virtual camera takes no ROI yet, so nothing needs it.
- **The camera's settings are functional properties,** each reading and writing the camera's configuration, so they can't drift from it; they aren't observable. `streaming`, `frames` and `last_frame` are data properties, and are.
- **The white balance is the camera Thing's setting,** saved in the data root, for the preview to apply (P1.14a): the camera itself has none, as its raw is linear.
- **The stream's frames go nowhere yet:** they are counted and let go, so the camera's pool doesn't run dry. The frame bus takes them in P1.14a.
- **Writing a setting while the camera streams is a 500, `busy`:** teta-wot has no 409 for a property but the global lock's.
- **A move waits up to 5 minutes for the stage,** and a cancelled move stops the stage before its invocation ends.
- **The Things reach the devices through the HAL's traits,** `Stage`, `Camera`, `ObjectiveMount` and `Illumination`, so the drivers of M5 plug in where the virtual devices are.

- **11 new tests:**

## Follow-ups and known gaps

- **The frame bus and the preview** take the camera's frames: P1.14a and P1.14b.
- **A `null` write to a property:** a teta-wot behaviour; if a Thing needs one, it is an upstream U-phase (ADR-0004).
- **A 409 for a device that is busy,** rather than a 500, would need teta-wot to let a property's error choose its status.
