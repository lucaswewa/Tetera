## Summary

`tetera-store`, a new L2 crate, keeps datasets on disk in projects ([ADR-0021](../../adr/0021-datasets-on-disk.md)): each dataset's frames in OME-Zarr v3 with OME-NGFF 0.5's metadata, compressed with zstd, beside its `metadata.json` and a JPEG thumbnail, all indexed in the data root's `tetera.db`. The phase's *Done when* holds:

- **A dataset's OME-Zarr reads back exactly, and its metadata follows OME-NGFF 0.5:**
  - raw Bayer in 8 bits and in 12 bits in 16, and grey in 8 and 16, each read back sample for sample;
  - the group's metadata checked against the spec's rules for multiscales: the version, the axes' kinds, order and units, one scale for each dataset;
  - the array's shape, its dimension names, and its zstd codec.
- **The index survives a restart, and lists no dataset abandoned while it was written:**
  - projects and datasets read back after the store opens again;
  - a writer dropped half way leaves no folder and no row;
  - a writer lost to a crash is cleared when the store next opens.

## What was built

- **`crates/tetera-store`,** L2, on `tetera-core` and `tetera-imaging`:
  - **`Store`:** projects (`create_project`, `projects`), datasets (`create_dataset`, `datasets`, `dataset`, `files`, `read`, `thumbnail`, `delete`), and the clean-up of a crash's datasets when it opens;
  - **`DatasetWriter`:** `append` a frame at a time, checked against the dataset's format and size; `finish` writes the metadata and thumbnail, cuts the array to the frames written, and lists the dataset; dropped unfinished, it removes its folder and its row;
  - **`DatasetReader`:** a dataset's metadata and frames, read back;
  - **`Index`:** projects, datasets and their files in SQLite, a dataset marked as being written until it is complete;
  - **the image:** array `0`, axes t, c, y and x, a chunk a frame, zstd at level 3; OME-NGFF 0.5's `multiscales` on the group, with the pixel size and the time between frames as its scale;
  - **the thumbnail:** each 2 × 2 Bayer cell as a pixel, at most 256 across, with a gamma of 2.2.
- **The jobs' history waits up to 5 s** for another connection's write to `tetera.db`, which it now shares.
- **The example `datasets`,** the crate's README, and ADR-0021.
- **New dependencies:** `zarrs` 0.23.14, released 2026-08-14, MIT or Apache-2.0, with only its `filesystem` and `zstd` features, and about seventy crates under it. Six of them had releases younger than two weeks, and are pinned to the last before 2026-11-14: `ahash`, `allocator-api2`, `walkdir`, `zstd`, `zstd-safe` and `zstd-sys`. zstd's C library is BSD-licensed.

## How to try it

```bash
cargo run --release -p tetera-store --example datasets
```

It writes 20 full-size raw frames into a new project, reads them back, prints the dataset's tree, and drops a second dataset half way. `-- --smoke` writes small frames.

## Performance

- **Writing 20 frames of 5472 × 3648, 12 bits in 16,** took 1.8 s, 449 MB/s of raw frames, compressing them with zstd on one thread as it goes. The example's frames are smooth and compress 149 : 1, far better than a camera's, whose noise leaves about 2 : 1; P1.15c's recorder, not the store, has to keep up with the camera.
- **A thumbnail** is made from the first frame on the CPU when the dataset finishes.

## Design notes and deviations from the plan

- **The spec's NGFF schema check is a reading of the spec's rules,** in the tests, not OME's JSON schemas, which aren't at hand offline. ADR-0021 lists it to watch.
- **A dataset plans its frames,** and one that ends with fewer is cut to them; it can't grow beyond its plan.
- **The datasets' folders are named by id,** so two datasets of one name can't collide; the name is in the index and the metadata.
- **The index shares `tetera.db` with the jobs' history,** each on a connection of its own that waits for the other's writes.
