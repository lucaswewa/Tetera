## Summary

Autofocus on the virtual instrument: P1.16a's search through the stage's z, each position measured on the first frame of the live stream exposed after the stage reported itself still, as a hardware job with the focus curve as its result, and the `autofocus` Thing. The phase's *Done when* holds:

- **In at least 95 of 100 trials at each objective, the error is a quarter of the depth of field or less:** 100 of 100 at 5×, its worst 0.18 d, and 100 of 100 at 50×, its worst 0.045 d, against the simulator's true focus, the section's middle, from starts within ±6 depths of field. Each of the other three measures, 25 trials at each, 25 of 25.
- **At 10×, it takes 5 s or less:** 3.00 s median of ten searches from 3 depths of field away, 3.04 s the longest.
- **A blank field fails cleanly:** with the sample unloaded, the curve is flat, the search finds no focus, the job fails `not_found`, and the stage is back where it started.

## What was built

- **In `tetera-workflows`, `autofocus`:**
  - **`autofocus(rig, request, step)`:** the depth of field from the objective fitted and the sensor's pitch; the search through the stage's z travel; the live stream started for it if it wasn't running, and stopped after, whatever happened;
  - **the instrument's probe:** a z move, x and y kept, that ends when the stage reports itself still, and a measure of the first frame exposed after that, through the frame bus's `first_after` (P1.14a);
  - **`AutofocusRequest`,** the measure and the search's options, each optional, and **`AutofocusResult`:** the search's `Focus`, the measure, the objective and the time;
  - **`depth_of_field`,** P1.16a's formula.
- **In `tetera-api`:**
  - **the `autofocus` Thing:** `autofocus` queues a search as a hardware job and answers with the job; the job logs the curve, and fails `not_found` when no focus is found; `last` keeps the last search's result, found or not. Refused at once without an objective fitted, or with options the search can't take;
  - **the instrument's sensor pitch,** for the depth of field;
  - **the example `autofocus`**.
- **No new dependencies;** `tetera-api` takes `tetera-imaging` as a dependency, no longer only for its tests.

## Performance

- **A search takes 26 positions:** 4.1 s at 5×, 3.0 s at 10× and 2.8 s at 50×, on the GPU. Each position is a z move at the axis's 2 mm/s and 20 mm/s², the stage's next report that it is still, up to 25 ms, and a frame exposed after it, within one of the live mode's 19 ms intervals and its exposure. At 5× the coarse steps are a depth of field, 31 µm, so the moves take longer.

## Design notes and deviations from the plan

- **The default stays normalised variance.** It met the spec at both objectives, as the literature expects. Brenner's gradient and the variance of the Laplacian were tighter at 5× on the simulator, though the Laplacian's barely rose above the noise on a small sensor (P1.16a). The real instrument's trials, in M5, should decide again; the measure is an option of each request.
- **The live mode's frames are measured as they stream,** with no mode switch: autofocus starts the stream if it isn't running, through the `camera` Thing's sink so its counts go on, and stops it after.
- **The x and y are kept** as the search found them, so a jog meanwhile isn't undone; the stage's own refusal of a move while it moves stops the search.
- **A blank field's job fails,** with the curve in its log and in `last`, rather than succeeding with no focus: a scan (P1.17) that autofocuses can tell at once.
- **The tests' CPU:** the virtual camera renders every frame of its live stream on the CPU, and its cost barely depends on the frame rate or the blur, so autofocus's tests starved the preview's and the captures' timed tests beside them, as a mutants run's baseline showed. A lock in each crate's tests now keeps them apart: autofocus's take it alone, the timed ones share it. The HTTP tests' instrument also runs in real time, not ten times as fast.
- **Known limit,** as for capture (P1.15d): the camera's and the stage's own actions aren't locked while a search runs.

## Tests, and the mutants

- **7 new tests:**
  - **on a small virtual instrument on the CPU:** the depth of field; focus found from below and from above, the stage left at it, the stream left as it was, running or not; every frame measured exposed after the stage was still; a blank field, no focus and the stage back; no objective, bad options and a search stopped by its step, each leaving the stream stopped;
  - **over HTTP:** a job that finds focus, its depth of field through the sensor's pitch, its result in `last` and its log; another measure by name; a blank field's job failing `not_found`, the stage back; refused without an objective or with bad options, no job made; a search cancelled through the `jobs` Thing, the stream it started stopped.
- **Broken on purpose,** as the spec asks: **the frame-after-t rule,** the newest frame measured whenever it was exposed: two tests failed, a frame exposed 135 ms before the stage stopped, and a focus found 2 µm off at 20×.
- **Run 15 times:** the three crates' tests, `tetera-workflows`', `tetera-api`'s and `tetera-jobs`', 75 in all, passed 15 of 15.
- **Mutants,** run with a timeout of 90 s rather than 60, as autofocus's tests render on the CPU:
  - **the first run,** over the change, 28 tested: 24 caught, 3 unviable, **1 missed:** the sensor's pitch, which no test of the Thing checked through the depth of field. It does now.
  - **The last run,** over the instrument: **3 of 3 caught.** Two runs before it found no baseline, as P1.14b's slow-client preview test failed beside autofocus's busy tests; the lock above fixed that.
