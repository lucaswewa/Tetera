## Behaviour spec

Written before the code, as a spec-first phase's must be. It covers P1.11b's tolerance and tiles too.

### What the sample is

A section of tissue stained with haematoxylin and eosin (H&E), on a slide, under a coverslip.
- **A function of position and depth:** at a point (x, y) on the slide, in µm, and a height z above the slide, in µm, it gives each stain's **density**: optical density per µm of the light's path (OD/µm).
- **An image is light passing through the section,** so a pixel's stain is its densities summed over the section's depth (P1.12 blurs each depth by its defocus).
- **Haematoxylin** stains nuclei blue-purple; **eosin** stains cytoplasm, collagen and red cells pink.
- **The mounting medium and the glass** absorb nothing here; the simulator's light adds its own background (P1.12).

### The section

- **Thickness** t: 4 µm by default, settable from 3 to 5.
- **Its lower face** lies at `z₀(x, y) = tilt_x · x + tilt_y · y`, 0 by default, so a tilt is a slope in µm per µm: 0.0005 is 0.5 µm per mm.
- **Inside** when `0 ≤ z − z₀ ≤ t`; outside it, nothing.
- **Folds,** when enabled: bands where the section lies doubled.
  - A band is where `|n_f| < 0.03`, n_f improved noise of wavelength 2 mm, so bands wander across the slide, about 40 µm wide.
  - Within a band the section is 2t thick. Its upper half is the tissue from 40 µm away in x and y, upside down: depth `2t − (z − z₀)` there.

### Where the tissue is

- **The tissue mask:** m, fBm of improved noise in x and y, 4 octaves from a wavelength of 800 µm. The weight `w = smoothstep(−0.10, −0.02, m)` takes it from bare glass to tissue over a few µm. Every stain density below is multiplied by w.
- **Regions:** within the tissue, two more noise fields choose what is where:
  - **glands** where `r_g > 0.20`, r_g improved noise of wavelength 450 µm;
  - **fat** where `r_f > 0.30` outside glands, r_f of wavelength 350 µm;
  - **stroma** elsewhere.

  Each is blended over a band of 0.05 of its field, so regions meet without seams.

### The tissue

All densities are in OD/µm.
- **Nuclei,** the haematoxylin:
  - **Placed** on a jittered grid of 10 µm cells in x and y: one candidate per cell, its presence, centre, shape and darkness all from the cell's hash.
  - **Presence:** 0.30 in stroma, 0.85 in a gland's epithelium, 0.06 in fat.
  - **Centre:** jittered within ±0.35 of its cell in x and y, at a depth from 0 to t in the section, so many nuclei are caps the section cut.
  - **Shape:** an ellipsoid with semi-axes a from 2.2 to 3.8 µm, b = a × 0.55 to 1.0, and c from 2.0 to 3.0 µm in z.
  - **Orientation:** along the local fibres in stroma, and across the ring in a gland's epithelium, as in columnar cells.
  - **Its edge:** where `q = (dx/a)² + (dy/b)² + (dz/c)²`, perturbed by 8 % noise, falls through 1. The density there is `smoothstep(1.0, 0.82, q′)`.
  - **Chromatin:** a granular texture from cellular noise of 0.7 µm cells, between 0.7 and 1.0 of the nucleus's density.
  - **Darkness:** 0.30 × (0.8 to 1.2) OD/µm of haematoxylin at most.
  - **A point's nucleus:** the darkest over the 3 × 3 cells around it.
- **Stroma:**
  - **Collagen fibres:** eosin 0.10 × (0.55 + 0.45 f). Here f is a sharpened stripe across the local fibre direction θ, with a period of 6 µm, wavered by noise.
  - **θ** comes from improved noise of wavelength 250 µm, so fibres sweep in bundles.
  - **Clefts,** pale gaps, where a noise field of 40 µm falls below −0.45.
- **Glands** in their regions, on cellular noise of 180 µm cells, one gland per cell:
  - each gland's radius is 0.30 to 0.42 of its cell, its edge perturbed by 6 % noise;
  - from its outside in: stroma; **the epithelium,** 16 µm thick, with its own cytoplasm and nuclei; **the lumen,** clear.
- **Cytoplasm** in the epithelium: eosin 0.07, haematoxylin 0.012.
- **Vessels** through stroma and fat:
  - their lumens lie where `|n_v| < 0.010`, n_v improved noise of wavelength 350 µm, about 12 µm across;
  - their walls are 3 µm beyond that, eosin 0.16;
  - **red cells** fill the lumens: discs of radius 3.6 µm, about 2 µm thick, on a jittered grid of 7 µm with presence 0.7. Each is eosin 0.40, paler at its centre by up to 35 %.
- **Fat** in its regions, on cellular noise of 70 µm cells:
  - membranes where the distance to the second-nearest feature point minus the nearest is below 1.2 µm, eosin 0.12;
  - clear within;
  - flattened nuclei now and then on the membranes, presence 0.06.

### Colour by absorbance

- **Stain vectors** (A. C. Ruifrok and D. A. Johnston, "Quantification of histochemical staining by color deconvolution", Anal. Quant. Cytol. Histol., 2001), each stain's optical density per unit amount in red, green and blue:
  - haematoxylin (0.650, 0.704, 0.286);
  - eosin (0.072, 0.990, 0.105).
- **A pixel's optical density** in each colour channel is the stains summed over its path, each times its vector.
- **Beer–Lambert:** transmittance `T = 10^(−OD)` in each channel; no stain is white.
- **For PNGs,** T is encoded with the sRGB transfer function to 8 bits.

### Procedural texture

- **Improved noise** (K. Perlin, "Improving noise", SIGGRAPH 2002):
  - gradient noise on the integer lattice, faded by `6t⁵ − 15t⁴ + 10t³`, with the 12 gradients toward the cube's edge midpoints;
  - each lattice point's gradient comes from an integer hash of its coordinates, the seed and a salt, not a permutation table, so no table is shared and the GPU computes the same;
  - its value is about −1 to 1, and 0 at every lattice point.
- **Cellular noise** (S. Worley, "A cellular texture basis function", SIGGRAPH 1996): one feature point in each cell, at a hashed position. F1 and F2 are the distances to the nearest and the second nearest, over the 3 × 3 cells around.
- **fBm:** octaves of improved noise, each at twice the frequency and half the amplitude of the last.
- **2D fields** extend through the section's depth, which is thin beside them; only nuclei and red cells vary in z.

### Hashing and determinism

- **The hash:** a 32-bit mix of the integer coordinates, the seed and a salt for each use, finished by C. Wellons's "lowbias32" (hash-prospector, 2018):

  `x ^= x >> 16; x *= 0x7feb352d; x ^= x >> 15; x *= 0x846ca68b; x ^= x >> 16`.

  A 64-bit seed is folded in, both halves. A hash becomes a float in [0, 1) as its top 24 bits × 2⁻²⁴, exact in single precision.
- **Coordinates:** a position is split into its lattice cell and the offset within it in double precision, then everything else is single precision. A slide is 100 mm, and single precision there would lose the 0.05 µm a 50× pixel needs.
- **Order:** each pixel's value is computed on its own, in a fixed order, so threads never change a result.
- **So:** the same seed gives the same bits for the same region on the same backend, whether rendered whole, in tiles, or on any number of threads.

### Rendering a region

- **A region:**
  - its origin on the slide, in µm;
  - its size in pixels;
  - its pixel size, in µm;
  - a range of depth;
  - a sampling.
- **Each pixel averages s × s points** on a grid within it. s is the smallest that keeps the points at most 0.25 µm apart, from 1 to 4, so a 5× pixel of 0.5 µm averages 2 × 2.
- **Depth is summed in layers of 0.5 µm,** each at its middle, over the range asked for:
  - an image in focus sums the whole section;
  - a depth slice is one layer, or a few.
- **It gives** each stain's optical density per pixel, and their transmittance in red, green and blue.

### Tiles and levels of detail (P1.11b)

- **A tile** is 256 × 256 pixels. It is keyed by its level ℓ, its column and row on the slide, and its depth layer.
- **Level ℓ** samples at 0.04 × 2^ℓ µm a pixel. A magnification's pixel size picks the level just finer, and the camera's sampling resamples from it (P1.12).
- **On the GPU,** a tile holds the two stains' densities in half precision, 256 KB a tile.
- **Tiles live in an LRU cache** within a VRAM budget from `tetera-compute`. A tile is made on first use; the least recently used goes when the budget is full.

### The backends' tolerance (P1.11b)

The GPU computes the same functions in single precision with CUDA's own `floorf`, `expf` and `sqrtf`. Results may differ in their last bits, and rarely a point falls across a lattice or cell boundary on one backend and not the other. So, for a region rendered on both:
- every pixel within 2e-3 OD, plus 1e-3 of its own value, on 99.99 % of pixels;
- no pixel beyond 0.05 OD.

Half precision's 11-bit mantissa adds at most 5e-4 relative, within that.

### What the tests check

- **Hashing:**
  - the same inputs give the same hash;
  - flipping any one bit of the seed changes, on average over many inputs, 14 to 18 of the hash's 32 bits;
  - floats fall in [0, 1), their mean about ½.
- **Improved noise:**
  - 0 at lattice points;
  - within −1.1 and 1.1;
  - continuous: a step of 1e-4 of its wavelength changes it by less than 1e-2;
  - mean near 0 over a large region.
- **Cellular noise:** F1 ≤ F2; F1 is 0 at a feature point.
- **The section:**
  - nothing below its lower face or above its top;
  - a tilt shifts it with x by tilt × x;
  - a fold doubles it within its band.
- **The tissue:**
  - bare glass where the mask is 0;
  - a nucleus's haematoxylin outweighs its eosin, and a red cell's eosin outweighs its haematoxylin;
  - a lumen is clear;
  - **depth ordering:** each nucleus is darkest at its own depth, and fades above and below.
- **Over a large region with the default seed:**
  - tissue covers 30 % to 85 % of it;
  - nuclei, counted by their centres, fall between 1,000 and 10,000 a mm²;
  - each of glands, fat, vessels and stroma is present.
- **Determinism:**
  - the same seed gives the same bits twice;
  - rendered whole or as four tiles, the same bits;
  - a seed one bit different changes most pixels.
- **Colour:**
  - no stain is white;
  - haematoxylin alone is blue-purple, blue the brightest;
  - eosin alone is pink, green the darkest;
  - more stain is darker.
- **Broken on purpose:** the seed's hashing, a bit of the seed dropped, which the test that every seed bit matters must catch.

## Summary

Tetera has a slide of H&E tissue, from a seed, that looks like tissue from a 2 mm overview down to 50×, and has depth. The phase's *Done when* holds:
- **The behaviour spec came first,** above, committed before the code.
- **The same seed gives the same bits,** whole or in tiles; a seed one bit different changes most pixels.
- **Depth orders as the spec says:** each nucleus is darkest at its own depth. The example's slices through one show it: 0.252, 0.272 and 0.254 OD at its middle pixel, the middle slice at its depth.
- **The PNGs are written** for the owner to judge at Checkpoint A.

What it has:
- **the section and its tissue:** a mask; stroma, glands and fat; nuclei, collagen, epithelium, lumens, vessels with red cells and fat cells; the section's tilt and folds;
- **colour by absorbance;**
- **rendering:** any region at any pixel size and depth, on all cores;
- **[ADR-0015](../../adr/0015-simulated-samples-on-the-gpu.md),** simulated samples on the GPU;
- **a golden test,** which pins the model's output, so the GPU twin (P1.11b) has a fixed reference.

## Performance

On 8 cores, a release build, the CPU twin takes about 0.04 µs a point in tissue, less on bare glass. A 512 × 512 tile at 50× takes about 17 ms; the 2 mm overview, 16 million points, 0.92 s. That is enough for tests and examples; the live view at 20 MP needs the GPU and its cache (P1.11b).

## Design notes and deviations from the plan

- **Two passes,** as ADR-0015 records:
  - nuclei and red cells are decided once for each grid cell, each at its own centre, so the listed nuclei and the rendered ones agree;
  - then each pixel is computed on its own.

  A nucleus's centre lies within its own cell, so a rectangle lists nuclei from exactly the cells it touches.
- **Improved noise in 2D,** with the 12 gradients of 3D less their z: every field but nuclei and red cells is the same through the section's depth, which is thin beside them.
- **Tuned after the first renders,** which were too pale, their tissue edges 40 µm soft, and their collagen soft bands at 50×. Against the spec above:
  - **the tissue mask's edge:** `smoothstep(−0.152, −0.148, m)`, a few µm wide and more of the slide covered, for the spec's `(−0.10, −0.02)`;
  - **vessels:** lumens where `|n_v| < 0.025` and walls to 0.034. The spec's 0.010 gave lumens 5 µm across, not 12;
  - **cytoplasm:** eosin 0.10 and haematoxylin 0.02, for 0.07 and 0.012;
  - **collagen:** eosin 0.16 × (0.45 + 0.55 f), for 0.10 × (0.55 + 0.45 f). Its stripes add fibrils of 1.7 µm to the bundles of 6 µm, and a grain of 0.8 µm;
  - **clefts:** where the noise falls through −0.44 to −0.46, crisper;
  - **nuclei:**
    - darkness 0.50 × (0.8 to 1.2), for 0.30;
    - densest at the centre, falling by a quarter toward the edge, so a nucleus fades above and below its depth, as the spec says;
    - a membrane, 30 % darker, as a ring around each depth's cross-section. As a shell, it darkened a nucleus's cap above its centre, against the spec's depth ordering, which its test caught;
    - chromatin 0.62 + 0.25 × clumps + 0.13 × grain, less like dots, for the spec's 0.7 to 1.0;
  - **red cells:** at depths from 1 µm to 1 µm short of the top, so each lies within the section.
- **A fold is the column continued through twice the thickness,** its nuclei spread through it, not the tissue from 40 µm away upside down, as the spec had it. It looks the same, a darker band of doubled section, for a fraction of the code.
- **The open fraction,** one less a vessel's lumen and wall, is clamped at zero: rounding left it a hair below, and a nucleus's likelihood −9e-9. The test that every column's densities stay in range found it.
- **The golden test pins the model.** It holds the fingerprints of seven regions that cover every part of it: their stains summed, plain and weighted by each pixel's place. They are compared within 1e-5 relative, a maths library's last bits. A deliberate change to the model records them again, and says so in its phase's notes.
- **PNGs by hand:** stored zlib blocks, uncompressed, rather than a dependency. The 5× field is 4.7 MB.

## Tests, and the mutants

- **20 new tests, 53 in the crate:**
  - **hashing and noise:**
    - the hash the same each time, and each of the 64 seed bits changing 14 to 18 of its 32;
    - units in [0, 1), their mean ½;
    - improved noise 0 on its lattice, within ±1.1, continuous, its mean near 0, as fine at 100 mm as at the origin;
    - fBm's octaves;
    - cellular noise's distances in order, and 0 at a feature point;
    - smoothstep both ways;
  - **the model:**
    - colour by absorbance: white, blue-purple, pink, darker with more;
    - a section's thickness and tilt checked, its tilt shifting it with x;
    - a fold doubling it within its band;
    - bare glass and each part of the tissue, the tissue covering 30 % to 85 %;
    - every one of 250,000 columns keeping its densities in range;
    - a rectangle listing the nuclei whose centres lie in it;
    - nuclei 1,000 to 10,000 a mm², each darkest at its own depth;
    - red cells in lumens, paler at their centres;
  - **rendering:**
    - the points a pixel averages;
    - bad regions refused;
    - the same bits twice, and whole or in four tiles;
    - a seed one bit different changing most pixels;
    - a nucleus darkest in the slice at its depth;
    - depth summed in layers, the last one partial;
    - colour following the stains;
    - **the golden regions as recorded.**
- **Broken on purpose,** as the spec asks: **the seed's hashing, its high half dropped.** Two tests caught it: "seed bit 32: 0 bits", and a seed one bit different changing none of 2,304 pixels.
- **Run 15 times,** the tests passed each time.
- **Mutants:** 750 in the phase's diff, with the virtual stage's tests skipped, since its code isn't in the diff.
  - **The first run missed 339 of 755.** Nearly all were the model's constants and arithmetic: a nucleus 0.30 + 0.12 × u of its cell, or 0.30 − 0.12 × u, is as plausible either way, and no property tells them apart. The golden test catches every such change.
  - **The second missed 25:**
    - untested: the range of a column's densities, which found the open fraction below zero; a partial last layer; bad regions with one side zero, or a depth range of nothing; the section as configured;
    - a listing's margin of cells, which found nothing more, now gone;
    - a grid's bounds check that could never fail, now gone.
  - **The last missed none:** 733 caught, 11 unviable, 3 timed out, each an endless loop.
  - **Seven exclusions, with their reasons in `.cargo/mutants.toml`:**
    - the shortcuts past a nucleus's or a red cell's edge, and for an absent region, which return early with what the code would give anyway;
    - ties at a fold's edge and between two feature points;
    - the walk around a grid's 3 × 3 cells, the same either way;
    - a last layer of no thickness.
