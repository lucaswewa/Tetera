## Summary

A stack of planes as one 3D array, and scans run, watched and steered over HTTP. A z-scan's dataset is a z-stack: its image's axes c, z, y and x, the planes along z, their step z's scale. The `scan` Thing previews a plan, runs a scan as a hardware job, pauses and resumes it, and says how far it has got. The phase's *Done when* holds:

- **The z-scan reads back as a 3D array:** a 21-plane z-scan at 10× read back whole as one array of 21 × 3648 × 5472, its planes 1 µm apart, from a dataset whose image is c, z, y, x in OME-NGFF 0.5's order.
- **A scan started through the `scan` Thing can be paused, resumed and cancelled, and says how far it has got:** over HTTP, a 3 × 3 snake paused after its fourth tile held at 44 % for a second, its job saying `paused`, and resumed to its end; in the tests, another cancelled through the `jobs` Thing while paused kept the tiles it took. `running` and each `progress` event say how far a scan has got.

## What was built

- **In `tetera-store`, a z-stack:**
  - **`DatasetSpec::z_step_um`:** a spec with a planes' step holds a z-stack; left out, frames in time, so datasets written before read as they did;
  - **its image:** OME-Zarr's array `0` with axes c, z, y and x, OME-NGFF 0.5's order of time, the channel, then space; one chunk a plane; the scale 1 on the channel, the step on z, and the pixel's size on y and x. A z-stack stopped early is shrunk along z, as frames in time are along t;
  - **reading back:** `read` gives a plane, as it gives a frame; **`read_stack`** reads every plane at once as one 3D array, z by y by x, and refuses frames in time. `frames` counts a z-stack's planes;
  - a step of 0 or less, or not finite, refused.
- **In `tetera-workflows`:** a z-scan's dataset is a z-stack, its step the pattern's.
- **In `tetera-api`, the `scan` Thing:**
  - **`preview`:** the plan a scan would make from where the stage is, its positions, grid, field of view, estimated time and timing; nothing moves;
  - **`scan`:** a scan as a hardware job after those before it, which the `jobs` Thing follows and cancels; its result the dataset, the plan, each tile's positions and the time. Refused at once without an objective fitted, with a request a scan can't take, a pattern beyond the stage's travel, or a project that isn't there;
  - **`pause` and `resume`** the running scan, refused when none runs; **`running`**, its job, how far it has got, what it is doing and whether it is paused; **`progress`**, an event for its every step; **`last`**, the last scan's result.
  - The HTTP tests that follow jobs share one `ended`.
- **The example `scan`** adds a 21-plane z-scan read back whole, and a snake through the `scan` Thing, paused and resumed.
- **No new dependencies.**

## Design notes and deviations from the plan

- **c, z, y, x, not z, c, y, x:** OME-NGFF 0.5 orders an image's axes time, then the channel, then space, so a z-stack's channel comes first, as a series' does after its time. Raw Bayer is one channel, so the planes are, in effect, a 3D array; `read_stack` gives them as one.
- **The z-stack's origin is in its frames' metadata,** each plane's recorded position, not in a translation in OME-NGFF's metadata: the spec asked for the step in the scale, and a viewer places planes by the scale alone.
- **The action is `scan`, not `start`:** a Thing's `start` is teta-wot's own, the call that starts it on the server; `scan/scan` reads as `capture/capture` and `autofocus/autofocus` do.
- **One scan's control a job:** the running scan's `ScanControl` lives while its job runs; `pause` and `resume` with none running are refused, `not_found`, rather than kept for the next.
- **`paused` in a progress event** is the scan asked to pause, so true from the step after the request; the scan holds at its next position and says `paused` there, again every 100 ms while it holds.
- **A module-wide allow** for clippy's count of arguments in `scan.rs`: the `scan` action takes a request's fields as HTTP gives them, and teta-wot's macro makes a function of them all, where an attribute on the action can't reach. The wrapper could carry the allow itself: a small fix for teta-wot's next release, among the follow-ups.
- **Known limit,** as for capture and autofocus: the camera's and the stage's own actions aren't locked while a scan runs.

## Tests, and the mutants

- **4 new tests,** and checks added to two:
  - **the store:** a z-stack of four planes of five planned, 16-bit Bayer and 8-bit grey: its array c, z, y, x, shrunk along z, one chunk a plane; OME-NGFF 0.5's rules and z's scale; each plane, and the whole read as one 3D array; frames in time refused as a stack; bad steps refused;
  - **over HTTP:** a plan previewed, nothing moved; a z-scan as a job, its every step an event in order with its share done, its dataset a z-stack read back whole, and `last`; a snake paused at its second tile, held, its progress still half a second on, resumed to its end; another cancelled through the `jobs` Thing while paused, keeping three or four tiles; `pause` and `resume` refused with none running; refusals without an objective, a project, or with a request a scan can't take, no job made;
  - **the engine's:** a z-scan's dataset a z-stack, a snake's not.
- **Broken on purpose,** as the spec asks: **the z step in the metadata,**
  - z's scale left at 1: the store's test failed, the scale `[1, 1, 0.24, 0.24]` against `[1, 0.75, 0.24, 0.24]`;
  - the engine's step left out: the engine's z-scan test and the HTTP z-scan test failed, the dataset "frames in time, not a z-stack".
- **Run 15 times:** the three crates' tests, `tetera-store`'s, `tetera-workflows`' and `tetera-api`'s, 93 in all, passed 15 of 15.
- **Mutants,** over the change, with a timeout of 120 s: 49 tested: 28 caught, 20 unviable, **none missed,** and 1 timeout: `resume` emptied leaves the paused test waiting to its end.
