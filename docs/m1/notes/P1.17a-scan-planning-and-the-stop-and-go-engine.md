## Summary

Scans planned and run stop and go on the virtual instrument: an XY snake and z-scans planned as lists of stage positions before they start, each with its estimated time; then, for each position, a move, the stage's report that it is still, an autofocus if the schedule says so, and the first full-resolution frame exposed a settle after, its position from the stage, written by a thread of its own into one dataset of tiles. Pause, resume and cancel are heard between positions and in every wait. The phase's *Done when* holds:

- **Recorded positions agree with the simulator's truth within the stage's repeatability:** each of the 25 tiles of a 5 × 5 snake at 10× recorded at the simulator's truth and at the plan, to a ten-thousandth of a micrometre, against the stage's step of 0.3125 µm.
- **A cancelled scan leaves a valid, partial dataset:** cancelled at the third and the sixth of six tiles, it keeps the two and the five taken, each read back; cancelled at the first, before any, it leaves none, and while paused it ends cancelled.
- **The estimated time is within 10 % of the time taken at 1×:** 11.95 s estimated for the 5 × 5 snake, 12.12, 12.02, 12.11 and 11.98 s taken in four runs, 0.3 to 1.5 % more. A fifth, straight after the release build, took 16.52 s, as WSL2's disk wrote slowly (see the manual test).

## What was built

- **In `tetera-workflows`, `scan`:**
  - **the planners,** pure functions of the request, the field of view and the stage's capabilities: `Pattern::Snake`, a rectangle covered with tiles of the field at an overlap, the grid centred on it so both edges are covered, rows along x in serpentine order; `ZRange`, a range and a step; `ZAround`, N planes a step apart centred on the current z; z-scans rising, so each plane is approached from below. Positions beyond the stage's travel are refused;
  - **`move_time`:** a move's time as the stage makes it, along the line at its most limited axis's speed and acceleration, a trapezoid or a triangle;
  - **`estimate` and `Timing`:** per position, the move, the stage's report, the settle, a frame, and an autofocus where due, or the write before it if that is longer; then the last write and the mode switch there and back. The plan carries its timing, so what the estimate is made of can be shown;
  - **`scan::engine`:** `ScanRequest` and its check; `AutofocusAt`, never, the first tile, or every N; `preview`, the plan from where the stage is, nothing moving; `scan`, the stop-and-go engine; `ScanControl`, pause and resume; `Tile`, each position planned, recorded and true, when the stage was still and when the exposure started; `ScanResult`.
  - **Capture's mode switch,** `to_full` and `restore`, shared with the scan.
- **In `tetera-hal`:** each axis's acceleration among the stage's capabilities; a camera mode's rate in 16-bit words beside its 8-bit one, and `CameraMode::fps(bit_depth)`.
- **In `tetera-sim`:** the virtual stage's accelerations and the virtual camera's rates in 16-bit words, as its own models already had them.
- **In `tetera-store`:** `DatasetWriter::append_as`, a frame written with metadata of the caller's: a tile's position as the stage recorded it.
- **In `tetera-api`, the example `scan`.**
- **ADR-0022,** scan planning and the scan engine.
- **No new dependencies.**

## Performance

- **A tile at 10× takes about 450 ms:** the move of 1.18 mm along a row or 0.79 mm to the next, at the stage's 20 mm/s and 200 mm/s², 154 or 126 ms; the stage's report that it is still, up to 25 ms; the settle, 50 ms; then the stream's next exposure, on average half its 138 ms interval at 12 bits, the exposure and its readout. Writing a full frame, about 300 ms, overlaps the next tile's move.
- **The estimate's history:**
  - **with a snap for each tile,** 14.5 to 14.9 s for the 5 × 5 snake against 9.4 s estimated, 35 to 37 % short: a snap at full resolution took 280 to 385 ms on the virtual camera, its readout and its rendering one after the other, where the estimate assumed a frame interval;
  - **with the stream,** each tile the first frame exposed a settle after the stage was still, and the estimate's frame half an interval to the next exposure, the exposure, and a readout of an interval: 11.95 s against 12.22 and 12.80 s, 2 and 7 % short;
  - **the interval at 12 bits:** a camera mode's rate was its 8-bit one, 14.49 frames a second in mode 0; at 12 bits, two bytes a sample, the virtual camera streams 7.24, as the MIChrome 20's link does. The mode now states both.

## Design notes and deviations from the plan

- **The settle holds by construction:** each tile is the first frame of the stream whose exposure starts a settle after the stage reported itself still, through the frame bus's `first_after` (P1.14a), rather than a sleep and a snap. A tile's time is the settle plus up to one frame interval.
- **The position is the stage's,** read while it is still and checked unchanged after the frame: for stop and go, the stage's track at the exposure's midpoint, as the spec asks. The frame's own metadata carries the simulator's truth, kept in each `Tile` for the comparison.
- **Writing overlaps moving:** frames go to a writer thread through a channel two frames deep. A writer that fails stops the scan, and is the error reported.
- **A cancelled or failed scan finishes its dataset** with the tiles taken. One stopped before its first tile leaves none: the store refuses to finish a dataset of no frames, and clears it.
- **Autofocus at a tile carries its z** to the tiles after, until the next. Only a snake autofocuses; a z-scan's request with autofocus is refused.
- **The estimate needs the disk's throughput,** 120 MB/s unless asked otherwise; the virtual stage's report period and latency, 15 ms in all, half the period and the latency; the switch to full resolution and back, 700 ms, as P1.15d measured them.
- **The tests' CPU:** the scan's tests stream on the CPU, as autofocus's do (P1.16b), so they share the tests' lock, and the one that autofocuses takes it alone.
- **A z-scan's dataset is still a stack of frames along time:** its 3D array is P1.17b's.
- **Known limit,** as for capture and autofocus: the camera's and the stage's own actions aren't locked while a scan runs.

## Tests, and the mutants

- **17 new tests:**
  - **the planners:** a snake's coverage, centring, serpentine order and either corner first, one tile for a rectangle smaller than the field, one more just over a whole step; z-scans by range, a step that doesn't divide it, and around the current z; bad patterns and positions beyond the travel refused;
  - **the times:** each move against the virtual stage's own profile, to a microsecond, in every direction; the estimate, each tile's longer of its work and the write before it, an autofocus, a slow disk; the plan's timing on the instrument;
  - **the request's checks at their edges,** and the scan's camera: the whole sensor at 12 bits, its exposure asked or the live view's;
  - **on a small virtual instrument on the CPU:** a 3 × 2 snake, each tile's position against the plan and the truth and its exposure a settle after the stage stopped, serpentine, and the dataset; a pause held until resumed, on the stage's clock; a cancel at the first, third and sixth tiles and while paused; a z-scan rising, its progress; an autofocus at the first tile, the rest at its focus; refusals, nothing moved;
  - **a camera mode's rate at each bit depth, and a frame written with the caller's metadata.**
- **Broken on purpose,** as the spec asks:
  - **the serpentine order,** every row left to right: two tests failed, the planner's at its fourth tile and the snake's, its second row's x rising;
  - **the settle wait,** each tile the stream's first frame after the stage stopped: the snake's test failed, a tile exposed 37.6 ms after the stage was still against a settle of 120.
- **Run 15 times:** the five crates' tests, `tetera-workflows`', `tetera-api`'s, `tetera-hal`'s, `tetera-sim`'s and `tetera-store`'s, 241 in all, passed 15 of 15.
- **Mutants,** run with a timeout of 120 s, as the scan's tests stream on the CPU:
  - **the first run,** over the change, 286 tested: 195 caught, 15 unviable, 16 timeouts, **60 missed.** The plan's timing, which no test checked beyond its estimate being above 0; the request's checks at their edges; the scan's camera configuration; the pixel size and the progress; a camera mode's rate; `append_as`; a z-range's step of 1 µm, which multiplies as it divides; a move's length, which cancels out of its time, so only a move backwards, its sum of doubled deltas negative, shows it. Each has a test now. One is equivalent and excluded with its reason in `.cargo/mutants.toml`: an axis that doesn't move counted among those that limit a move, its limits along the line infinite.
  - **The last run,** over the missed mutants' functions: 104 tested: 99 caught, 4 unviable, **1 missed:** an autofocus position's move in the estimate, a depth of field up, turned down, which takes as long. It is now a move from 0 to a depth of field, and over the plan's preview after that, **36 of 36 caught**.

## Gate

`cargo xtask gate phase`: **G1 pass**, on 2026-11-28, at `a7d472d`, with the CUDA steps. The Python step is skipped until M2.

The run before it failed its stress step, each test binary on two CPUs, three at once: the scan's test that autofocuses timed out twice, waiting 5 s for a frame. In a scan, autofocus measures the full-resolution stream, and a full frame of the tests' small sensor took a second or more to render on two loaded CPUs in a debug build; the camera drops frames when it falls behind, so a frame came within about two renders. Autofocus now waits 10 s for a frame, as a scan's tile does.
